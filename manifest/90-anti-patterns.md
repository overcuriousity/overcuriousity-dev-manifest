# 90 · Anti-patterns

## Tells of a generated UI

- NEVER: tinted boxes with a thick coloured left stripe.
- NEVER: gradient text, gradient buttons, purple-to-indigo gradients.
- NEVER: emoji as icons or in headings.
- NEVER: an icon in a coloured rounded tile on every card heading.
- NEVER: glass or blur panels.
- NEVER: big rounded cards with soft drop shadows.
- NEVER: giant "stat" tiles.
- NEVER: pill badges on everything.
- NEVER: everything centred.
- NEVER: verbose, narrating code comments.
- NEVER: marketing microcopy.

## Other

- NEVER: native `confirm()`, `alert()` or `prompt()`.
- NEVER: CDN assets, unconditional egress or telemetry.
- NEVER: `unsafe-inline`, `unsafe-eval`, inline styles or inline scripts.
- NEVER: literal colours outside the token file.
- NEVER: text below 12 px or a px root font size.
- NEVER: a hamburger menu as primary phone navigation.
- NEVER: custom pickers on touch or native pickers on desktop.
